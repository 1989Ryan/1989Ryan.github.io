Zirui Zhao

ziruiz [at] comp.nus.edu.sg

Zirui Zhao (赵子瑞) is a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at National University of Singapore. He is advised by Prof. Lee Wee Sun. He also works closely with Prof. David Hsu. His research interest is the intersection of robotics and machine learning, and its application in long-horizon task and motion planning.

Before joining NUS, he received his Bachelor's Degree in Automation Engineering at Qian Xue Sen Program, Xi'an Jiaotong University. During his undergraduate study, he worked with Prof. Ding Zhao in Safe AI Lab@CMU and Prof. Pengju Ren in Cognitive Architecture Lab@XJTU.

Active Learning for Risk-sensitive Inverse Reinforcement Learning
Rui Chen, Wenshuo Wang, Zirui Zhao, Ding Zhao
Preprint, 2019
code / arXiv

Risk-sensitive inverse reinforcement learning provides an general model to capture how human assess the distribution of a stochastic outcome when the true distribution is unknown (ambiguous). This work enables an RS-IRL learner to actively query expert demonstrations for faster risk envelope approximation.
